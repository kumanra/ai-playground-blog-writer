# Anti AI Writing

Read anti_ai_writing_instructions.md if available.

Remove:
- generic AI openings
- robotic transitions
- fake importance
- corporate wording
- overused AI phrases
- perfect but lifeless paragraph rhythm

Rewrite with:
- simple language
- short paragraphs
- specific examples
- natural human flow
- clear opinion
